Our pharmacy

Behind every script: real pharmacists

Every prescription from your Dr Buttons consultation is dispensed through our partner network of Australian-owned community pharmacies. A registered pharmacist personally reviews your script, checks it against your history, and offers you counselling before anything leaves the building. It's the same standard of care you'd get at the counter of your local pharmacy, delivered to your door instead.

  • Pharmacist review and counselling on every script
  • Available for medication questions throughout your treatment
  • A text to confirm your order and a secure payment link, no account to set up
  • Plain, discreet packaging with full tracking
